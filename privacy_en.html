Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026
hovvy ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting and appropriately managing the personal information and user data of all users across our web services and smartphone applications ("Apps").

1. Handling of Data in Mobile Applications

This section applies to Android mobile applications distributed by hovvy (including "ParallelCook", "NiceShot Log", "Daily Brain Training", "Atom-Q", etc.).

✓ Completely Free (No In-App Purchases): All features are provided entirely free of charge. We do not offer paid subscriptions, and we do not collect or store credit card or payment information.

✓ No Account Required & No External Server Storage: No account registration or sign-in is required. All user-generated data is stored exclusively on your local device and is never transmitted to our external servers.

✓ Banner Advertisements Only: To support ongoing development and maintenance, our apps display banner advertisements served by Google AdMob.

1.1 Data Collected and Stored

  • Locally Stored Data: All data entered or created within the app (such as recipe information, cooking steps, golf practice logs, score histories, and preferences) is stored solely on your device. We do not access, view, or store this data on our servers.
  • Advertising Information: To deliver banner ads and measure ad performance, third-party advertising modules (Google AdMob SDK provided by Google LLC) may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and non-personally identifiable diagnostic information.

1.2 Advertising Services

Our applications display banner advertisements via Google AdMob. To learn more about how Google handles user information, please visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you prefer not to receive personalized ads, you can opt out at any time through your Android device settings ("Settings" > "Google" > "Ads") or via the in-app ad settings.

1.3 Data Deletion

You can permanently delete all data stored locally on your device by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's storage data via your device settings.

2. Handling of Information on Our Website

2.1 Contact Inquiries

Personal information (such as your name and email address) submitted through our website contact form is used strictly to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you. We will not disclose or provide this information to third parties except when required by law.

2.2 Web Analytics Tools

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand traffic and improve user experience. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous traffic data. You can refuse the use of cookies by adjusting your browser settings.

3. Security Measures

We implement reasonable and appropriate technical and operational security measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or leakage of personal information.

4. Revisions to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time in response to changes in legal regulations or our service offerings. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
