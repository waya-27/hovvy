Areatta? Privacy Policy

1. Basic Policy

"Areatta?" (hereinafter referred to as "the App") is a smartphone application designed to simply record and manage household inventory and shopping lists.
The App is completely free, ad-free, requires no account registration, and performs no external data transmission.

2. Information Collection and Handling

The App does not collect, acquire, or transmit any personal information such as your name, email address, device identifiers, usage logs, or location data.

  • Item Data (Names, Stock Status, etc.): All memo data entered into the App is stored exclusively on your device's local storage and is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.

3. External Transmission and Third-Party Provision

The App does not include any third-party ad SDKs, analytics tools, or tracking modules. No user data is shared with or provided to third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All memo data resides solely on your device. Uninstalling the App from your device will permanently delete all stored data.
