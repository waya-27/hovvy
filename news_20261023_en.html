October 23, 2026

Notice Regarding Restructuring of Application Operations

To ensure our customers can use our services with complete confidence, hovvy has restructured its application operations with the highest priority placed on security and quality maintenance.
We are focusing our efforts on applications that users can rely on safely and over the long term, with the following changes.

Consolidation to Android Versions

We are consolidating our mobile app offerings exclusively to Android (Google Play) and have ended distribution of iOS versions on the App Store.
By focusing on a single mobile platform, we will be able to deliver updates and security enhancements much more swiftly.
Previously installed iOS apps will no longer receive future updates or support.

Discontinued Applications

  • Nobishiro
  • Short Block
  • Areatta?
  • Arealist
  • Prompt Beacon

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to everyone who has used these applications.

Application Temporarily Suspended for Renewal

  • Shumi no Shirushi
  • Un-AI

This service will be rebuilt with a safer architecture and relaunched in the future.
We will post an update here when it resumes.

Review of Continuing Applications

We will continue to provide "ParallelCook," "NiceShot Log," "Atom-Q," and "Daily Brain Training."
To make these apps safer and easier to use, we will be reviewing their features and interface structures sequentially.
Details will be announced in upcoming news posts as preparations are finalized.

Future Initiatives

We will continue to strengthen our security measures while actively working to create services that everyone can enjoy safely and over the long term.
